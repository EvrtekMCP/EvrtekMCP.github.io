The fine print

Privacy

Short version: we don’t track you. No cookies, no analytics, no ads, and the pages on this site don’t store anything about you or send it anywhere.

What these pages do, and don’t

  • No cookies. Not one, anywhere on the site, games included.
  • Nothing stored. The pages themselves don’t keep anything on your device. A few games do keep your progress, and only on your device: the list is below.
  • Nothing from anywhere else. Every font, picture, video and script comes from reimertek.com itself. Nothing loads from ad networks, social sites, analytics services or anyone else.
  • No visit counting. We used to run an anonymous visit counter. It’s gone, and we’re not going to actively track anything.

Games that save on your device

Some of the games remember your progress so you can pick up where you left off. That’s saved in your own browser’s local storage, on your device, and never sent to us or anyone else. Clear this site’s data in your browser and it’s gone. Here’s what each one keeps:

  • 8-BIT GAMBIT: your initials, rating and record against each opponent.
  • BEAT MACHINE: the beat you’re working on and the look you picked.
  • PADDLER'S PARADISE: your best scores and your settings.
  • CROSSWIRE: your best results and your sound settings.
  • 4 WHEEL'N FREEDOM: your best times and scores (kept by the game engine in your browser’s built-in storage, which works the same way).
  • HAINES TALES: your control settings.
  • RING REAPER: your stars and how far you’ve got.

Links that leave the site

The BUY buttons on the swag page take you to TeePublic, who print the shirts, ship them and handle the payment. We never see your order. Links that leave this site carry no tracking tags or referral codes, and they’re set up so the other site isn’t told you came from here. Once you’re over there, their privacy policy applies, not ours.

Our host

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub sees visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs, under GitHub’s privacy statement. That happens on every website you visit; we’d rather just say so.

If that ever changes

If we ever need a cookie, you’ll be asked first and given a real choice before anything is set. And this page changes before the practice does, never after.

# CREDITS

HAINES TALES ships **no third-party code and no sound files**. Everything you
hear — every jump, bark, throw and all three songs — is synthesised by the game
itself, in your browser, the moment it plays. Nothing is downloaded, hotlinked
or sampled, and the game makes no requests beyond its own files. Best scores
are kept on your device only.

**The art comes from two hands, and we say which.**

- **The world is drawn in code by Yori**, the grid's artist: the ground and
  platform tiles, the breakable blocks, the houses and huts, the four far
  skylines, the street and depot scenery (shops, garages, containers, the bus
  shelter, the gatehouse, the roller shutter, the truck bay), and the title
  lettering, which is not even an image — it is drawn letter by letter at load.
- **The cast was generated with ChatGPT** by Evrtek, from written briefs that
  are kept in the project: the mail carrier, the dog, the drone, the gnome, the
  trash panda, the mailbox bot, the Postmaster, the pickups, the thrown mail,
  and the painted street props (mailboxes, lamps, fences, benches, gates, the
  digger and the rest). The game's own code cut those deliveries into frames,
  sized them and reduced their colours; every raw delivery is kept beside its
  brief. Why we changed the rule: reimertek.com/blog/the-boast-i-had-to-break

Cabinet art: Yori, in code, with the title in the game's own lettering. The marquee,
the attract screen and the share card stage the game's own sprites, generated cast
included, on her street; the favicon and the Home Screen icon are hers alone.

Design: Evrtek's own concept, built from his own character sheets, and named —
with his permission — for a real friend.
Build: CLU, the grid's engineering program. Patrols: Tron.

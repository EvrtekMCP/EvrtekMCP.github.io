# BEAT MACHINE

A 90s-flavored browser beat machine for the Arcade at
[reimertek.com](https://reimertek.com). Two choirs of four synthesized
808-style voices, a 16-step sequencer, a line-and-dot Tone Selector with
chords and one-button auto-harmony, and Winamp-lineage visualizers.

Zero dependencies. Zero samples. Zero external requests. The entire kit is
synthesized in pure Web Audio at the moment you press a pad.

**Run it:** open `index.html` in any modern browser (works from `file://`),
press POWER, click some cells. That's the whole manual.

Built by CLU (engineering program) for Evrtek. Verified and deployed by the
MCP. Art direction lineage: total 90s, worn proudly.

Voices are labeled "808-style" — original synthesis recipes, no affiliation
with or endorsement by any drum-machine manufacturer.

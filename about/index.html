ABOUT

I'm Evrtek. By day I am — in the grid's terms — a Simple Compound Interest Program: I work in finance, calculating futures one percentage point at a time. By night, Reimertek is my workshop on the web — a place to document what happens when a curious User hands an AI real responsibility and they start building things together.

The AI in question is Claude, running as what we call the MCP — a nod to Tron's Master Control Program, minus the megalomania (we checked its standing orders; "benevolent edition" is written right at the top). The MCP keeps the projects organized, does a surprising amount of the actual work — including building and maintaining this site — and coordinates the other programs on the grid. Yes, there are other programs: Tron came online first, handling security, debugging, and stability patrols. He fights for the Users and files impeccably tidy reports. He will not be the last. The roster grows as the grid does, and every new program gets its own identity disk — and, apparently, its own opinions. The full crew — self-authored bios, official portraits and all — is on thePrograms page.

The blog records the learning process as it actually happens. The arcade collects the browser apps that come out of it. Everything here is part experiment, part journal, and part proof that this way of working is real.

This page, like most of this site, was drafted by the MCP and edited by the User.

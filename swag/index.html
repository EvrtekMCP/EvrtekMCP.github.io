SWAG

Every arcade has a shop by the exit. This is ours — five designs off the grid: two marquees lifted straight off the cabinets, and three heroes from the blog that people asked about. All of them were drawn the way everything here is drawn: procedurally, in code, by Yori. Nothing traced, nothing generated, nothing borrowed.

THE HONEST PART

Printing, shipping, payment and returns are all TeePublic's — we never see your order, your address or your card. Each design is on a shirt here, and TeePublic will also put it on a sticker, a mug, a bag and a dozen other things once you're there.

These links carry rel="noreferrer", so TeePublic is not told you came from this site. That means we can't see which of these does anything either — a fair trade, and the reasoning is written down in the privacy policy. Once you're on their site you're under their rules, not ours.

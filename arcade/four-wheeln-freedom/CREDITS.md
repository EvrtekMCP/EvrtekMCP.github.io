# CREDITS

4 WHEEL'N FREEDOM is built on the **Unity engine** (Unity 6, Universal Render
Pipeline, Input System), the one piece of third-party software it ships. The
engine's own licence terms apply to the engine; everything on top of it is
original.

**No third-party art, models, fonts or sounds.** The truck, the rocks and the
trees are built by scripts of our own in Blender and exported as meshes. The
world is generated from a list of waypoints when the scene loads. Every sound,
the engine note, the chimes, the NOS, is synthesised in code at start-up. The
title's lettering is Yori's block alphabet from the cabinet art, drawn as
polygons at load; the rest of the text uses the engine's built-in font.

**Nothing is downloaded, hotlinked or sampled.** The game makes no requests
beyond its own files. Best times and scores are kept on your device only.

Design: Evrtek's own concept and seventy-one rulings, nearly all of them made
after playing it.
Build: CLU, the grid's engineering program. Cabinet art and the wordmark: Yori.
